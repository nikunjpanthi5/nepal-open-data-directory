# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Nepal Open Data Directory a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, personal appearance, background, experience, education, nationality, religion, or identity.

We value constructive discussion, accurate attribution, transparent data practices, and collaboration.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate.
- Welcoming different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focusing criticism on ideas, evidence, and code rather than people.
- Respecting privacy, attribution, licenses, and data protection.
- Correcting mistakes openly and in good faith.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private or confidential information are not acceptable.

Deliberately misrepresenting a dataset's source, license, or contents is also contrary to this project's standards.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the repository maintainer through GitHub's private contact options. Include relevant links or context where possible. Reports will be reviewed and handled as fairly and confidentially as practical.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, project spaces, and when someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove comments or contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and communicated where appropriate.
