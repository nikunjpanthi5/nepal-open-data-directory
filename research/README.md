# Research resources

A curated index of research-oriented datasets, benchmarks, papers, code repositories, and data portals relevant to Nepal.

## Current status

No research resources have been reviewed and published yet.

## What belongs here?

- Research datasets released by universities, laboratories, and research projects.
- Nepali language and speech corpora, OCR datasets, and NLP benchmarks.
- Public geospatial, climate, health, agriculture, and socioeconomic research data.
- Reproducible research repositories that clearly document data access.
- Papers that provide a dataset or a verifiable route to obtain it.

A paper is not itself a dataset. Label publications, code, and data separately.
