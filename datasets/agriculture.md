# Agriculture datasets

Crops, livestock, land use, irrigation, food systems and agricultural statistics for Nepal.

## Discovery leads from Open Data Nepal

The titles below were found in the [Open Data Nepal agriculture collection](https://opendatanepal.com/dataset?groups=agriculture). They are **discovery leads, not fully reviewed directory entries**. Verify each original publisher page, resource URL, metadata and license before creating a formal catalogue record.

1. National Sample Census of Agriculture of Nepal Data (2001/02, 2011/12 and 2021/22)
2. National Wheat Research Program Agronomy and Soil Experiments Data Package (FY 2067/68–2080/81)
3. Department of Food Technology and Quality Control Annual Bulletins
4. Irrigation, Land Use and Crop Yield Data from the Irrigation Master Plan
5. Department of Agriculture Annual Progress Reports
6. Department of Agriculture Yearbook Data
7. National Wheat Research Program Annual Reports
8. National Centre for Fruit Development statistics on fruits, coffee, tea and flowers
9. National Centre for Fruit Development Annual Progress Reports
10. Madhesh Province District Agriculture Offices Data

## Publish a reviewed entry

Use the [dataset entry template](../CONTRIBUTING.md#dataset-entry-template). Include the original publisher, source page, format, coverage, exact license and date last checked.

A public download link does not automatically grant permission to redistribute a file. Link to the original source unless redistribution terms are clear.
